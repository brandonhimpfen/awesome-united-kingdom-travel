## Official & Planning

Essential official government entry portals, tourism boards, electronic travel authorizations, and weather forecasting.

- [VisitBritain (Official British Tourism Portal)](https://www.visitbritain.com/) - Official national tourist board guide featuring destination highlights, regional itineraries, and travel inspiration.
- [UK Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) Portal](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/apply-for-an-electronic-travel-authorisation-eta) - Official UK Government portal for applying for mandatory pre-travel entry permissions.
- [Met Office Weather](https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/) - Official national meteorological service providing regional weather forecasts, severe weather warnings, and climate tracking.

## Transportation & National Rail

Navigating national railway networks, underground transit, coach services, and aviation hubs.

- [National Rail Enquiries](https://www.nationalrail.co.uk/) - Official gateway to Britain's national rail network, timetables, live departure boards, and ticket operators.
- [Transport for London (TfL)](https://tfl.gov.uk/) - Official municipal transport authority managing the London Underground (Tube), buses, Elizabeth line, and river services.
- [TfW (Transport for Wales)](https://tfw.wales/) - National rail and transport operator connecting Welsh regions and border routes.
- [British Airways](https://www.british-airways.com/) - National flag carrier operating international long-haul flights and domestic routes through Heathrow (LHR) and Gatwick (LGW).

## Destinations & Regions

### London & England

- [Visit London (Official City Portal)](https://www.visitlondon.com/) - Official municipal visitor guide for exploring the West End, museums, royal parks, and historic landmarks.
- [VisitEngland](https://www.visitengland.com/) - Official regional tourism portal for exploring historic cathedral cities, countryside villages, and coastal destinations.

### Edinburgh & Scotland

- [VisitScotland (Official National Portal)](https://www.visitscotland.com/) - Official national tourism guide for exploring Edinburgh Castle, the Highlands, lochs, and historic distilleries.

### Cardiff & Wales

- [Visit Wales (Official National Portal)](https://www.visitwales.com/) - Official national tourism guide for exploring Cardiff Castle, Snowdonia (Eryri) National Park, and coastal paths.

### Belfast & Northern Ireland

- [Discover Northern Ireland](https://discovernorthernireland.com/) - Official regional travel guide for exploring Belfast, the Titanic Quarter, and the Giant's Causeway.

## Heritage & National Parks

Resources for exploring protected historic sites, ancient castles, and national parks.

- [National Trust](https://www.national.trust.org.uk/) - Official conservation charity protecting historic houses, gardens, industrial monuments, and coastline across England, Wales, and Northern Ireland.
- [Historic Environment Scotland](https://www.historicenvironment.scot/) - Official agency managing Scotland's historic monuments, castles, and archaeological properties.

## Safety & Emergency Services

Practical advice on emergency contacts, medical assistance, and traveler safety protocols.

- [Emergency Services (Police, Fire, Ambulance - Dial 999 or 112)](https://www.gov.uk/) - Direct nationwide emergency dispatch numbers operational across all UK regions.
- [Police Non-Emergency Line (Dial 101)](https://www.police.uk/) - Official contact line for reporting non-urgent police matters and inquiries across England, Wales, and Scotland.
